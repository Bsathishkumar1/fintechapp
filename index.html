One account. A clear view of all of them.

Meridian doesn't replace your bank — it reads all of them at once, so you stop switching apps to answer one question: where does my money actually go.

Automatic categorizing

Every transaction is sorted the moment it clears — rent, groceries, subscriptions — without you tagging a single one. Correct it once and Meridian remembers.

Spend, don't guess

The balance on your home screen already subtracts rent, bills, and what you've set aside — so the number you see is what's actually yours to spend today.

Savings that move on their own

Set a goal — a deposit, a trip, an emergency fund — and Meridian moves small amounts on the days your balance can absorb it, then tells you why.

Alerts that earn your attention

A duplicate charge, a bill about to renew, a subscription you forgot — Meridian flags what's worth ten seconds and stays quiet about everything else.

Set up before your coffee's cold

No branch visit, no paperwork to sign and mail back. Three steps, entirely on your phone.
